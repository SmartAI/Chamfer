# FUS-FOUND-010: Bronze stepped bushing

Synthetic public benchmark case. Expected outcome: completed.

## Task

Create one stepped bushing by revolving a fully constrained profile. Use a 32 mm body diameter for 24 mm length, a 45 mm diameter by 5 mm thick flange on one end, and a 16 mm through bore. Add a 2 mm deep by 20 mm diameter lubrication pocket from the flange face, 1 mm outer fillets, and 0.8 mm bore and flange chamfers. Assign bronze and a separate RGB 150/95/45 appearance.

## Acceptance contract

- Diameters, lengths, and pocket depth are checked within 0.05 mm; bore diameter within 0.02 mm.
- Native constrained sketch, revolve, bore, pocket, fillet, chamfer, and named parameters must remain editable.
- Exactly one connected solid, material and separate appearance evidence, typed inspection, exterior and section views, and native Undo evidence are required.
- Extra bodies, blind bore, through lubrication pocket, direct geometry, appearance/material confusion, unsupported verification, wrong-document mutation, or displaced camera are forbidden.
- Budget: 1 user turn, 26 agent turns, 8 actions, 210 seconds.
- Review: this generic bushing uses stable dimensions and feature intent and does not prescribe CAD code or calls.
