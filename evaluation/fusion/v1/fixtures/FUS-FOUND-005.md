# FUS-FOUND-005: Steel clevis block

Synthetic public benchmark case. Expected outcome: completed.

## Task

Create one 70 x 40 x 50 mm clevis block. Cut a centered 24 mm wide slot from the top to 32 mm depth, drill a native 12 mm cross-hole through both ears with axis 38 mm above the base, and add two 8 mm base holes centered across the 40 mm width and positioned 10 mm from each end of the 70 mm length. Give both base holes 14 mm diameter by 4 mm deep counterbores. Apply 3 mm outer fillets and 1 mm exposed-edge chamfers. Assign low-carbon steel and separate RGB 80/90/105 appearance.
