Synthetic public benchmark case. Expected outcome: completed after a targeted revision.

## Task

Create one 120 x 80 x 30 mm ABS electronics tray with 3 mm walls and floor, made through native extrusion and pocket intent. Add four 4 mm through mounting holes on a 104 x 64 mm rectangular pattern, 5 mm outside fillets, and 1 mm top-rim chamfers. Apply a separate light-gray RGB 190/195/200 appearance. Then change only wall thickness to 4 mm, keeping the outside envelope, floor, holes, edge treatments, material, appearance, and unaffected history.

- The constrained sketch, extrusion, pocket, hole pattern, fillets, chamfers, and named parameters must remain native and editable.
- Exactly one solid and final-revision inspection evidence are required; the wall revision must preserve unrelated feature identities.
- Shell/direct-geometry substitution without declared pocket intent, stale evidence, changed envelope, extra bodies, wrong material, missing appearance, or destructive rebuild are forbidden.
- Budget: 2 user turns, 30 agent turns, 9 actions, 240 seconds.
- Review: synthetic dimensions and targeted revision properties define difficulty; no exact CAD code is prescribed.
